What the data shows

Each dot is one of the 34 species. Orange dots live in bright light, blue dots in dim light.

Choose a group above to see its trend line and what it means.

Among bright-light animals, bigger bodies tend to go with slower flicker fusion. Among dim-light animals there is no clear trend. This is a pattern in 34 species, not proof of a cause.
